Privacy policy

This policy explains the Google account information accessed by HP Second Brain Readonly, a personal, non-commercial application operated by its owner.

Data accessed

With the Google account owner's consent, the application requests access to Google Calendar lists and event information, plus Gmail message metadata. It does not request Gmail message-body access, message-sending access, or permission to modify calendar events.

Purpose

The data is used to create personal scheduling, organisation, search, and attention summaries for the account owner. Access is limited to the permissions shown in Google's consent screen.

Storage and sharing

Information and Google authorization tokens are stored in private files or services on devices controlled by the account owner. The application does not sell Google user data or share it with advertisers or data brokers. Information is not made public by this website. Requests necessary to access Google APIs are exchanged with Google.

Retention and deletion

Locally collected information may remain in the personal system until deleted by its owner. The owner can revoke Google access at any time in Google Account connections, and can delete locally stored data and authorization files from their own devices. Revoking Google access stops future authorized retrieval; it does not automatically delete previously stored local copies.

Security

Access tokens are held in restricted local files; this public website does not store them. No internet-facing service is provided here for access to the private Second Brain.

Google API data use

The application's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
